Prime Loxodrome

Primes at p−s, s = ½ + it, on the Riemann sphere

Walk in the plane

Nearest zero γ– t − γ– Circle radius 1/|t−γ|– |W(N)|– ln N (line length at γ)–
Prime points sit at p−s = p−1/2e−it ln p: a logarithmic spiral in the plane, which stereographic projection turns into a loxodrome winding into the pole 0 (bottom).
The walk adds the arrows Λ(n)/√n·e−it ln n with the pole term of ζ removed. Between zeros it circles; at a zero it runs straight out from 0. A straight line through 0 maps to a meridian, so at t = γ the walk climbs a meridian towards ∞ (top).
